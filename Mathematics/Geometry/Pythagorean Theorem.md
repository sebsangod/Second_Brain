## Description

the sum of the squares on the legs of a right [triangle](https://www.britannica.com/art/triangle-musical-instrument) is equal to the [square](https://www.britannica.com/science/square-mathematics) on the _hypotenuse_ (the side opposite the right angle) or, in familiar algebraic notation:

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$
