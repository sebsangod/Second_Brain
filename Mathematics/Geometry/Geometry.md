## Description

_Geometry_ is a branch of ``mathematics`` **concerned with properties of space such as the distance, shape, size, and relative position of figures.**

Originally developed to model the physical world, _geometry_ has applications in almost all sciences, and also in art, architecture, and other activities that are related to graphics.

_Geometry_ also has applications in areas of ``mathematics`` that are apparently unrelated. For example, methods of ``algebraic geometry`` are fundamental in [Wiles's proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiles's_proof_of_Fermat's_Last_Theorem "Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem") of [Fermat's Last Theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_Last_Theorem "Fermat's Last Theorem"), a problem that was stated in terms of [elementary arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_arithmetic "Elementary arithmetic"), and remained unsolved for several centuries.
