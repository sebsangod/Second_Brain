## Description

It is part of _infinitesimal calculus_ (which studies _functions_ when they undergo very small changes, close to zero) and _mathematical_ analysis.

The _differential calculus_ **studies how continuous** _functions_ **change when their variables undergo infinitesimal changes**.

The main subject of study in _differential calculus_ is the _derivative_ (or rate of infinitesimal change). An example of this is the calculation of the instantaneous velocity of a moving object.
