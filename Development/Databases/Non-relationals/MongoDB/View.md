## Description

A ``MongoDB`` _view_ is a **read-only queryable object** whose contents are defined by an _aggregation pipeline_ on other ``collections`` or ``views``.

``MongoDB`` **does not persist the view contents to disk. A view's content is computed on-demand when a client queries the** _view_.
