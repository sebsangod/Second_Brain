## Description

A _database_ is an **organized collection of structured information or data**.

Typically, a _database_ is managed by a _database management system (DBMS)_. Together, these components are referred to as a _database system_.

Today, data is usually organized as **row-and-column structures in a series of tables** to increase the efficiency of data processing and querying.

This makes it easy to **access, manage, modify, update, control, and organize the data**.
