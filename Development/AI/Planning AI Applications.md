## Details

### Use Case Evaluation

The first question to ask is **why you want to build this application?**. Like many business decisions, building AI applications is often a response to risks and opportunities, like:

- _If you don't do this, competitors with AI can make you obsolete_. **If AI poses major threat to your business**, incorporating AI must have the highest priority.
- _If you don't do this, you'll miss opportunities to boost profits and productivity_. Most companies embrace AI for the opportunities it brings. **AI can help in most, if not all, business operations**.
- _You're unsure where AI will fit in your business yet, but you don't want to be left behind_. While a company shouldn't chase every hype train, many have failed by waiting too long to take the leap (cue Kodak, Blockbuster, and Blackberry).


#### The Roles of AI and Humans in the Application

What role AI plays in the AI product influences the application's development an its requirements [Apple](https://oreil.ly/Dz1HE) has a document explaining different ways AI can be used in a product.

Involving humans in AI's decision-making processes is called _human-in-the-loop_.


### Setting Expectations

Once we've decided we need to build this amazing AI application by ourselves, **the next step is to figure out what success looks like: how will we measure success**. The most important metric is **how this will impact my business?**
