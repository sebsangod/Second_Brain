## Description

_Data_ is **the most important part of any** ``AI`` **model**.

If we have **poor** _data_, the ``training`` **results will be poor**, and so will the _model’s_ final performance.

If we have a **good initial** ``dataset``, one that is diverse and relevant to the use case or the ultimate goal, the **predictions will be more accurate**.
