## Description

_TypeScript_ is a **strongly typed programming language** that builds on ``JavaScript``, giving you better tooling at any scale.

_TypeScript_ **adds additional syntax to** ``JavaScript`` to support a **tighter integration with your editor**. Catch errors early in your editor.

_TypeScript_ **code converts to** ``JavaScript``, which **runs anywhere** ``JavaScript`` **runs**: In a browser, on ``Node.js``, Deno, Bun and in your apps.

___
