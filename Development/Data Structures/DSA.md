## Description

Programmers use `data structures` **to improve the speed and strength of** `algorithms`. In computer programming, this combination is known as _DSA_ for _"data structures and algorithms"_.

_DSA_ helps programmers address the twin challenges of `time complexity` and `space complexity`.
