## Details

### Specific
Instead of just said _I want to improve my English_, the methodology makes us think in a specific goal like:
<h1 style="text-align: center;">I'm going to get a C2 level</h1>

### Measurable
How will I achieve my goal?:
<h1 style="text-align: center;">I'm going to make the level test every 3 months</h1>

### Attainable
Be realistic, how much time can I study per day or per week constantly?
<h1 style="text-align: center;">I'm going to study 3 days a week</h1>

### Relevant
What is the real impact this goal will bring to my life?
<h1 style="text-align: center;">This goal will open every door and bring me a lot of great opportunities</h1>

### Time-bound
When will I achieve my goal?
<h1 style="text-align: center;">I am going to achieve my goal in 1 year</h1>
